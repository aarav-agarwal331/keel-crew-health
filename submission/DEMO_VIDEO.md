# 30-second demo video: script and storyboard

Record at tablet size (820 × 1180, dark theme) from the live site. Screen recording only, captions
burned in, no voice needed (add the voice-over line if the format allows audio). Every shot is a real
screen; the path to reach it is given. Total 30 seconds.

Before recording: open the site, go to **More → Erase my data on this device** so the demo starts
clean, then **More → Mission clock** and drag to **Flight day 101** (a day with a working link).

End card (last second, over shot 6): **Keel — a crew health monitor that keeps working when Earth cannot answer.**

Notes for whoever records it:

- Shot 2 must land before 0:10. Do not trim shot 1 below three seconds; the problem statement needs it.
- The replay in shot 3 is the scripted illness of crew member Lucía Reyes on the simulated Mars
  transit: symptoms are scripted on flight day 120, during a two-week link blackout. Keel flags it on
  the day symptoms begin, from the symptoms she ticks at check-in combined with drifting readings.
  The sensors alone show the illness pattern one day after symptoms begin. The day before, the
  detector had moved but read the change as low mood, and that is not counted. Do not describe the
  result as early warning; the validation does not support that claim.
- The caption in shot 6 must keep "with thresholds set only in simulation" and "No useful early
  warning; most alerts are false" on screen together with the result.
- On a clean start the Today screen shows the strip "Keel is a crew health monitor that works with
  no link to Earth" and the button "Demo: replay a scripted event (40 s)". The strip is a good
  opening frame if you prefer to start there; dismiss nothing.
- Every screen that shows mission data carries the "Simulated data" chip. Keep it in frame.
- A phone-width recording also works; the layout is the same in one column.
