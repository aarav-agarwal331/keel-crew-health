"Steady" and "no flags" mean within the person's own usual range and no rule triggered. They do not mean the values are healthy.

The tier is a rule-based summary: it is never better than the worst area on the crew member's own screen, and nausea or clearly slowed reactions always mean a conversation first. It is not a validated fitness-for-duty test and not a clearance.
