Sharing and privacy

Lucía Reyes, your health data is yours. You decide below what each role can see.

Always visible, whatever you choose below
To the commander: your readiness tier (no Keel flags / minor flags / talk to the medical officer first), without the reason. A commander has to be able to plan a spacewalk safely.
To the crew: counts. When three or more crew members show a change in the same area, the Crew screen shows the count, not the names. In a crew of four that is not fully anonymous.
To everyone: cabin readings (air, radiation). They are not personal.
Emergency sharing is on
When on, a red-flag symptom or an "act now" safety reading is shown at once to the medical officer and to the commander as backup. Agreed before flight; you can switch it off.
