Sharing and privacy

Lucía Reyes, your health data is yours. You decide below what each role can see.

How it travels: in this demo every role is on one tablet. In a flight version your data stays on your tablet, and only the views allowed here would go over the cabin network, to the medical officer's and commander's tablets; the daily ground summary goes out when a link exists.

Always visible, whatever you choose below
To the commander: your readiness tier (steady / worth a look / talk to the medical officer first), without the reason, except emergencies (see below). A commander has to be able to plan a spacewalk safely. The tier is worked out from all your readings, including ones you keep private.
To the crew: counts. When three or more crew members show a change in the same area, the Crew screen shows the count, not the names. In a crew of four that is not fully anonymous.
To everyone: cabin readings (air, radiation). They are not personal.
Emergency sharing
On
Off
When on, a symptom you mark as serious or an "act now" safety reading appears at once on the medical officer's Crew screen (nobody is alerted, so tell them), and stays on their list until they close it with a note. The commander is shown it too, as backup, except the items marked "medical officer only" at check-in (thoughts of harming yourself). Emergency sharing sends no details to the ground: the daily ground summary carries only what you set for the flight surgeon below, plus a count of urgent items still open, so the flight surgeon knows to call. Every fixed safety rule (a reading or a serious symptom) that needs attention now or today goes on the medical officer's list; notices from your readings follow the sharing levels below; for an area you have not shared, they see only that there is an item, not what it is. If you switch this off, both can see that it is off.
