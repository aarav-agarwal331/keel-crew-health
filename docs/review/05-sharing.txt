How it travels: in this demo every role is on one tablet. In a flight version your data stays on your tablet, and only the views allowed here would go over the cabin network, to the medical officer's and commander's tablets; the daily ground summary goes out when a link exists.

Always visible, whatever you choose below
To the medical officer: an overall status made only from the areas you share with them, and, under emergency sharing, every open "act now" or "act today" item from a safety rule, with what it is and the readings behind it, in any area. Notices from your readings follow the levels below.
To the commander: your readiness tier (steady / worth a look / talk to the medical officer first), without the reason, except emergencies (see below). A commander has to be able to plan a spacewalk safely. The tier is worked out from all your readings, including ones you keep private. An item marked "medical officer only" can make it "Talk to the medical officer first", but the commander is never shown why.
To the crew: counts. When three or more crew members show a change in the same area, the Crew screen shows the count, not the names. In a crew of four that is not fully anonymous.
To everyone: cabin readings (air, radiation). They are not personal.
