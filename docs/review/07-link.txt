Simulated with simplified geometry (circular, coplanar orbits). On this outbound leg the delay grows from seconds to about 6 minutes. After arrival it keeps growing: Earth to Mars is about 4 minutes at closest approach and about 24 minutes at the greatest distance.

Why this matters

On the International Space Station, flight surgeons in Mission Control watch crew health and cabin air in real time and talk to each crew member privately every week. NASA's own planning documents say that on a Mars mission delays and blackouts will end reliable real-time telemedicine, and that medical decisions must move on board.
