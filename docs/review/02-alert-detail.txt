Take your temperature with the thermometer and hold exercise until you have. Tell the medical officer.

PUBLISHED RESEARCH

In our own test on Earth data the resting heart rate rise during illness averaged about 3 bpm and usually appeared on or after the first day of symptoms; one published study reported larger, earlier rises in the cases it detected. Immune function changes in space and latent viruses reactivate in most ISS crew. NASA lists hygiene, quarantine and monitoring as countermeasures.
