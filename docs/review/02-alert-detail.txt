PUBLISHED RESEARCH

On Earth, wearables showed resting heart rate rising a median of 7 bpm about 4 days before infection symptoms. Immune function changes in space and latent viruses reactivate in most ISS crew. NASA lists hygiene, quarantine and monitoring as countermeasures.
