PUBLISHED RESEARCH

In our own test on Earth data the resting heart rate rise during illness averaged about 5 bpm at its peak, a day or two after symptoms began, with wide differences between people: three in ten showed no rise. Immune function changes in space and latent viruses reactivate in most ISS crew. NASA lists hygiene, quarantine and monitoring as countermeasures.
