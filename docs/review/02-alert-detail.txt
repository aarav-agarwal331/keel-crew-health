PUBLISHED RESEARCH

In our own test on Earth data, resting heart rate rose by about 4 to 5 bpm on average over the four days from symptom onset (4.6 bpm in the people the simulator was not fitted on; individual peaks were higher). People differed widely, and some showed no rise at all. Immune function changes in space and latent viruses reactivate in most ISS crew. NASA lists hygiene, quarantine and monitoring as countermeasures.
