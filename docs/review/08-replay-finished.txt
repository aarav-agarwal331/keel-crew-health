RESULT OF THIS REPLAY

Keel flagged this on the day symptoms began, with a specific action and no link to Earth.

Earlier in this replay Keel also raised 3 notices that the scripted event did not cause. Some reflect ordinary lapses in the simulated routine, such as a missed exercise session; some are false alerts. That is part of the picture: notices are budgeted at about one false one per person-month.

Across all the real data, not this one script: Keel flagged 67% of 103 real illness episodes at one false alert per person-month, 31 points more than luck. It did not give early warning. Everything except illness is tested in simulation only.

One scripted event proves nothing by itself.
