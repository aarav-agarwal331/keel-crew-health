Simulated with simplified geometry (circular, coplanar orbits). On this outbound leg the delay grows from seconds to about 6 minutes. After arrival it keeps growing: Earth to Mars is about 4 minutes at closest approach and about 24 minutes at the greatest distance. Next scripted blackout: Day 119.

Why this matters

On the International Space Station a flight surgeon is seconds away by voice, talks privately with each crew member about once a week, and the ground team follows exercise and cabin-air data. Routine vital signs are not streamed to the ground, so Keel proposes more self-monitoring than ISS crews have today, on purpose. NASA's own planning documents say that on a Mars mission delays and blackouts will end reliable real-time telemedicine, and that medical decisions must move on board.
